Safe by design

It never touches your games.

Syntra only uses normal Windows features that change what your monitor shows. Every version is built in public, and you can check your copy is the official one from inside the app.

Phone control

Change your colours from your phone.

Sign in on your phone and move the sliders — your PC screen follows instantly, on any Wi-Fi or mobile data.

  1. Open Syntra on your PC and sign in.
  2. Scan the QR code in Settings → Control from anywhere.
  3. Sign in on your phone and drag. That's it.
